Well things have changed quite a bit since I last wrote on here--I hope to make this website a routine. That guitar amp I got back in March sounds amazing and I have continued playing guitar unlike previous excursions where I don't stick with the hobby/skill. It is lovely how music can make you feel. If I am having a slow, somber, or sad day I play my emotions out on that guitar, through that amp. Same for when my day is fast, exciting, and stimulating. Although, I have been making more than just sound waves lately. I joined a new lab at Stanford University. Still doing pain neuroscience as I was at UCSF. I am excited for the new learning environment. My first day was this Monday, August 31st. Still to early to say how it is apart from navigating HR and lab trainings. 

For this new position I moved from San Francisco down the Peninsula to Menlo Park. It is definitely a slower speed, quieter neighborhood, older average resident age than SF. I am adjusting. Yes, adjusting. From my studio apartment that only gets early morning sunlight I can here the CalTrain horn. More than I expected, the sound of that train horn makes my blood pump and heart lift. It reminds me theres a connected world out there. Afterall, it can take me straight up to SF in about an hour. When I lived in SF, I could hear the sound of the Muni trams stopping right outside my door. It is something I didn't realize I had grown accustomed to. In the evenings sometimes I bounce around not certain what I want to do. I play some guitar then stand and drink water, reaching for the next task. I sit on my stool and stare out the window. Spinning into my next charrade, I sit and play guitar. I am sort of unsure what to do with those evenings, that were spent walking along Ocean Beach, bobbing down to the club, or just shuffling into my favorite restaurant. I am...adjusting. 

In the mornings I eat yogurt, drink water, and get on my bike. I bike to the Stanford lab and start my day. Still trying to get access to the lab building which is an ordeal, so I usually wait and text someone to let me in. Things will pick up once I have gotten past the new lab hurdles. I can feel it.
